# Your passes

**Passes** lists your passes with the balance left, the validity period and a line
saying **"as of …"**.

That line is part of the balance. It says when your device last saw anything new.
If you have not been in a studio for three days, the number is three days old —
and the app claims nothing else.

![Your passes](/img/screens/en/passes.png)

## Where the number comes from

It is not stored, it is calculated from the individual entries: the purchase, then
each redemption. Every entry is signed by the device that wrote it. That is why
your phone and the desk at the second location arrive at the same number
independently.

You can read your passes but not change them — not even your own balance.
Whoever took the payment keeps the books.

## You carry the news

Your phone carries your pass from location to location. Check in at the old town
and walk over to West, and that desk knows about it as soon as you come in —
because **you** brought it, not because there is a server somewhere.

![A contradiction on a pass](/img/screens/en/fork-alarm.png)

## Backup

**Export my passes** downloads a file with your entries, signatures included.
That is your receipt if something ever comes apart. It is not required — your
studio holds the same entries — but it costs nothing.

## A contradiction on a pass

If a pass shows a contradiction, two counters redeemed the same class without
knowing about each other. Only **one** unit is deducted regardless. It is shown so
that somebody can sort it out.
