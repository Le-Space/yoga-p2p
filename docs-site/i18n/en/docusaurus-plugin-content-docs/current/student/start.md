# Getting started

You need no account and no email address.

## Open the app

It runs in the browser: through the QR code in the studio, or directly at
[yoga.le-space.de](https://yoga.le-space.de). On a phone you can add it to the
home screen, after which it behaves like an app.

## Create a passkey

On first opening, your device creates a **passkey**. Not a password you can forget
or hand over — a key that stays on the device and is unlocked with a fingerprint,
your face or a PIN.

The identifier can be anything you like. **An email address is not required**, and
without one you give away less.

<div class="no-server">

**Keep the passkey safe**
If your phone syncs passkeys with your Apple or Google account, you will find
yourself again on a new device automatically. If it does not and the phone is
lost, your identifier is gone — your studio can move the remaining balance to a
new one, but only for as long as **it** still has the old pass.

</div>

## Connect to the studio

At the desk: one device shows a QR code, the other scans it. After that you see
the programme, can book, and can look at your passes.

That connection is not a login. It only says where your devices can find each
other — see [Connecting](/connecting).
