# Selling a pass

Under **Till**. The student has to have been connected to this device before, or
they are not in the list.

1. Choose the student
2. Choose the pass
3. **Cash received**

That is all. There is no second step and no confirmation from anywhere.

## What happens

The sale is written into the student's pass account as an entry, **signed by this
device**. That signature is the receipt: it says which device at which location
took the money, and it can still be checked years later.

No balance is stored anywhere. It is calculated from the entries every time —
which is why two locations can reach the same answer without consulting each
other.

## If a sale hangs for a moment

Right after a first pairing a sale can take a few seconds. That is not a fault:
permission to write into the pass account has to reach this device first. The app
waits and keeps trying rather than refusing a sale that is about to be allowed.

## Moving a pass to a new phone

When somebody loses their passkey, their identifier is gone — the balance they
paid for is not.

1. The student sets up their new phone and connects it to you.
2. Under **Till → Move a pass to a new DID**, choose old and new.
3. **Move balance**.

The remaining balance moves to a new pass and the old one is voided. Classes
already used do not come back, and the validity period stays as it was — a
transfer replaces a pass, it does not sell one.

<div class="no-server">

**If a message appears**
Should the app report that the old pass could not be voided, the balance now
exists **twice**. Repeat the transfer until the message goes away. The app says so
rather than hiding it — only a person can resolve that.

</div>
