# Yoga-P2P

Class booking, passes and check-in for a yoga studio with more than one location —
no server, no account, no monthly fee.

There is no central machine holding your data. It lives on your devices: the iPad
at the front desk, your colleague's phone at the second location, your students'
phones. When two of those devices meet, they bring each other up to date.

<div class="no-server">

**This is the difference**
You do not need an internet connection to check somebody in. You do not need an
account to book. And nobody outside your studio can read your class lists — there
is no place where they come together.

</div>

## What that means day to day

**It works when the internet is down.** Check-in only needs the student's phone
and your device to be in the same room.

**There is nobody to call.** No provider who resets a password or restores a lost
account. That is why this handbook keeps coming back to second devices and
backups — not small print, but the price of nobody looking over your shoulder.

**Some things you find out later.** If two counters work without a connection at
the same time, a pass can be redeemed twice. The app does not prevent that — it
**shows** it, with both records, as soon as the devices meet again. More under
[What the app cannot do](/what-it-cannot-do).
